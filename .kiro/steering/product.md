# Product: Last Rites

**Last Rites** is a first-person 3D survival-horror mystery that runs in the browser. It is being built for the Kiroween 2026 hackathon, **Costume Contest** category, where judges score a haunting, polished UI whose spooky design *supports how the game works*.

## Pitch
You are the new gravekeeper of Hollowmere Cemetery. The previous keeper, Elias Thorne, vanished, and the dead won't stay buried. By day, in a graveyard with nothing living in it, you search the grounds for materials and clues and solve small puzzles. By night the curse holds back the sun, and you survive endless waves until you can name the truth at the altar. Solve three cases from Hollowmere's past, finish the rite, and walk out at sunrise, or the cemetery takes you.

## Pillars (every feature must serve at least one)
1. **Dread over gore.** Darkness, sound and anticipation do the work. Little blood, no gratuitous violence.
2. **Spooky UI is functional UI.** Every visual effect carries information: the health bar drains with a visible chunk of recent damage, the lantern flame is your oil, the glow on the workbench shows what's craftable, the map is hand-drawn and fills in as you explore.
3. **Curiosity is rewarded.** Exploring finds materials, lore, keys and clues. The cases pull the player forward, and every answer follows from clues in the game alone.
4. **Fair scares.** Every jumpscare is telegraphed by a flicker, creak or silence. Players can set the intensity.
5. **Finishable scope.** One cemetery, three zones, three stages. Polish beats size.

## Core loop
Day (explore, collect, investigate, craft) → Dusk (bell tolls, prepare, set traps) → Night (waves of zombies, scares in the alleys) → Dawn (new recipe, next journal chapter, pick an upgrade, visit the merchant). Three stages, each one day plus one night, each a case solved through puzzle minigames. By day there are no zombies. At night the curse holds back the sun and the waves never end until the case is answered at the chapel altar; the win is all three cases solved, then a walk out of the gates at sunrise. Dawn: next case's clues, pick an upgrade, visit the merchant.

## Target
- Desktop browsers (Chrome, Edge, Firefox) on mid-range laptops, keyboard and mouse.
- Judges will play for about 5–10 minutes. **The first 2 minutes must impress**: atmosphere, a first scare and a first craft within reach. A Short Vigil option (3 stages, 2-minute days) fits a full run in about 15 minutes, and the case board offers hints so nobody gets stuck.

## Out of scope
Multiplayer, mobile/touch controls, procedural maps, user accounts, monetization.
