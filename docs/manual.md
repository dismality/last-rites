# Last Rites: Instruction Manual

*Hollowmere Cemetery, October 1891.*

You are the new gravekeeper. The last keeper, Elias Thorne, vanished, and the dead won't stay buried. By day you search the grounds for materials and clues. By night you hold out against the dead until you can name the truth at the chapel altar. Solve three cases from Hollowmere's past, finish the rite, and walk out at sunrise.

> This manual has no spoilers. For the answers, see the [walkthrough](walkthrough.md).

---

## 1. Getting started

- **Play** in a desktop browser with a keyboard and mouse.
- **Click the game** to lock the mouse. Press **Esc** to free it and pause.
- **New Game** asks for **Normal** or **Developer mode** (see section 10). **Continue** loads your latest save.
- **The intro** can be skipped with **Space** or **Enter**.
- **On your first day**, a short tutorial teaches the basics as you play.

## 3. The screen

- **Objective (top left).** Your current goal. A **pillar of amber light** stands over the place it means, with its name and distance.
- **Compass (top).** It shows the same marker, plus anything nearby worth knowing about.
- **Health bar (bottom left).** Below 30 it pulses and shows a "Low" tag. Health only comes back from bandages or the altar.
- **Lantern flame.** This is your **oil gauge**. As the oil burns down, the flame and your circle of light shrink.
- **Stamina.** Sprinting and kicking use it.
- **Clock.** By day it counts down: "Dusk in m:ss".
- **Controls hint.** A small panel above the health bar teaches the controls a few at a time: first moving, looking, sprinting, the lantern and using things; then the journal, satchel, items, map and pause; at dusk, the fighting keys. Each line fades once you've used it.
- **Journal key.** When an objective needs the journal (a question to answer, the handwriting to compare, the rite's order), its key shows under the objective.
- **The keeper speaks.** Short lines in the keeper's voice say what a find means. Press **Space** (or click the line) to skip to the next one.
- **Outlines.** Things you can use or pick up get an outline when you look at them.

## 4. How a run works

The game has **three stages**. Each stage is one day, one night and a dawn.

### Day
- **Nothing can hurt you by day.** The graveyard is empty of anything living.
- **Explore** to gather materials, read gravestones, pick up clues and solve the stage's puzzles.
- **Craft** before dusk. You start with only a rusty shovel.

### Dusk and night
- **The curse holds back the sun.** The dead rise in waves, and the waves **never stop** until you answer the case at the chapel altar.
- **The lantern burns oil** from dusk onward, so pick up oil wherever you see it.
- **Stay near the chapel.** Nail boards over its doors to keep the dead out. Damaged boards can be repaired between waves.

### Dawn
- **Pick one upgrade** from three on offer. Dawn waits until you choose.
- **Silas Vane, a travelling undertaker,** parks his cart by the south gate for the first 90 seconds. He sells supplies for Grave Coins.
- **New recipes** unlock, and fresh side contracts are dealt.

## 5. Solving a case

1. **Follow the objective.** Each stage is a short chain of steps: read these stones, check this record, pull these bell cords. The objective names the step and a **pillar of lantern light** marks the place. The italic line under it says why it matters, and when a step is done a short line says what you learned.
2. **Solve the puzzles.** Some steps open small puzzles, such as checking the parish ledger against the stones or putting a torn watch log in order. Everything you learn is written up in your journal (**J**, the **Case** tab).
3. **Answer the question.** Once the steps are done, the Case tab asks the case's question, such as "Who silenced John Pell?". Type a name.
4. **Give the answer at the altar.** Go to the chapel altar **at night** and press **E**.
   - Done early? Once every step is finished, the altar's bell can **call the dusk now** for a small score bonus.
   - Listen for the warnings: a far bell tolls two minutes before dusk, and the graves tremble at thirty seconds.
   - **Right:** the waves stop, the dead fall to dust and the sun rises.
   - **Wrong:** a wave arrives at once and the altar sulks, silent, for 30 seconds. Then you can try again.

**Some things are red herrings.** Not every clue points at the truth. A few things lie about the grounds to mislead you; the journal's write-up says which finds matter.

**A name keeps turning up?** It is meant to. Hollowmere's three cases are one family's story.

### Hints
Each case has three hints that you buy with Grave Coins: which cards matter, then the rule, then the answer. In the story modes, the last hint becomes **free** after the night has run for 3 minutes, so you can never be stuck for good.

## 6. Survival

### Your lantern
- **The lantern is your light.** The less oil it has, the smaller your circle of light.
- **Some things show only in lantern light.** Keep it lit at night.
- **Refill it** with lamp oil, or with a crafted Lantern Refill.

### Fighting
- **Light attack (left mouse):** quick swings.
- **Block (right mouse):** take less damage from the front.
- **Heavy attack (V):** hold to charge. With an axe or sabre, a charged blow knocks enemies down.
- **Kick (Q):** shoves a zombie back and staggers it.
- **Dodge (C):** a quick sidestep out of harm's way.
- **Guns:** right mouse to aim, left mouse to fire, **R** to reload. Gunshots are **loud** and draw the dead toward you.
- **Low enemies:** look **down** at a creature on the ground before you swing, or use a heavy attack to chop down at it.

### Weapons you can find
- **Rusty shovel:** your starting weapon. It also digs graves.
- **Wood axe:** at the woodpile.
- **Service revolver** and **double-barrel shotgun:** each is hidden somewhere on the grounds.
- **Silver sabre:** later in the game.

Crafted weapons wear out with use. Your shovel never does.

### Thrown and placed items
- **Holy Water Flask:** stuns and burns the dead.
- **Nail Bomb:** a big blast after a 2-second fuse.
- **Lantern Trap:** bursts into flame when something comes near.
- **Salt Circle:** for 30 seconds, nothing can cross it.
- **Tallow Candle:** lights an area and makes scares less likely nearby.

### If you die
- **Retry Night** starts that dusk again.
- **Retry from morning** starts that day again.

Either way, you start at full health with at least 60% oil. Clues, journal pages and solved puzzles stay found.

## 7. Crafting

- **Where:** craft at the **workbench** in the keeper's hut, which is straight ahead when you wake.
- **What you can make glows.** The rest show what they're missing.
- **The world keeps running** while you craft (1.5 seconds), so crafting at night is a risk.
- **Recipes** unlock as you go: at dawns, from journal pages, and when you pick up guns (for ammunition).

### Starting recipes

| Make | From |
|---|---|
| Bone Club | 3 bone, 1 cloth |
| Bandage (heals 30) | 2 cloth |
| Tallow Candle | 2 candle wax, 1 cloth |
| Lantern Refill (+50% oil) | 1 lamp oil, 1 candle wax |

### Materials
Bone, iron nails, candle wax, holy water, cloth, lamp oil, grave salt, wood planks, black powder, lead shot and silver filings.

Look in disturbed graves, broken coffins, fences, the hut, the chapel, crypt shelves and the catacombs. Dig a grave by holding **E** over it with the shovel.

### Satchel (Tab)
- **12 slots.** Each stack of materials or ammunition and each crafted item takes one slot.
- **Keys, relics and Grave Coins** take no space.
- **The hotbar (1 – 4)** holds whatever you want to hand.

## 8. The dead

| Creature | What to know |
|---|---|
| **Shambler** | Slow, and comes in groups. The common foe. |
| **Lurker** | Waits still in dark alleys and niches, then lunges when your back is turned or your lantern is low. |
| **Wailer** | Keeps its distance and screams, calling every zombie nearby to you. Kill it first. |
| **Bloater** | Bursts in a cloud of grave gas when it dies, hurting anything close, including other zombies. Kill it at range. |
| **Brute** | Very tough. Light hits only stagger it. Use heavy attacks or a close shotgun blast to knock it down. |

**Elites** are stronger versions of the common dead. They drop better loot and more coins.

**Other things walk Hollowmere too.** Each one you meet is added to the **Creatures** page in your journal, with a field note and what works against it. Read it.

### Scares
Hollowmere is a horror game. Each scare is **telegraphed**: you get a warning sound or sign before it happens. Every important sound also shows a subtitle. You can turn scares down or off in Settings (see section 11).

## 9. Coins, upgrades and extras

- **Grave Coins:** the dead drop them. Spend them on hints, at Silas Vane's cart, and at the vending machine in the hut.
- **Side contracts:** small goals such as "Dig 3 graves" or "Nail 3 barricade boards", paid in coins. New ones are dealt each dawn.
- **Upgrades:** one at each dawn. They include more health, faster reloads, slower oil burn, a bigger satchel, faster digging and stronger boards.
- **Score:** each kill scores points. Kills close together build a **combo** up to ×3. Clearing a night unhurt earns a bonus.
- **Achievements:** listed on the title screen.
- **Saving:** the game saves itself at every dawn and every dusk.

## 10. Modes

| Mode | What it's like |
|---|---|
| **Normal** | The three stages and the rite, as intended. |
| **Developer mode** | The same game, with two helpers: **]** skips the current step, and **`** opens the developer panel, where you can jump to any stage. Handy if you only have a few minutes and want to see the later stages. |

Developer mode can also be switched on or off any time in **Settings**.

## 11. Settings

- **Volume:** master, music, sound effects, ambience, voice and strange noises. Each is separate; set strange noises to 0 to turn them off.
- **Scares:** scare intensity (Full / Reduced / Off) and jumpscares on or off.
- **Comfort:** flashing lights, camera shake, head bob and field of view.
- **Reading:** subtitles and a plain journal font.
- **Graphics:** a Low setting for slower computers.
- **Controls:** rebind any key except Pause.

## 12. Tips from the last keeper

- **Read everything.** Gravestones, tags, papers and journal pages all matter.
- **Craft a weapon before your first dusk.** If dusk is close and you only have the shovel, the objective will remind you.
- **Grab the oil you see on the path every day.**
- **Board up the chapel doors before the first wave.**
- **When you're lost, look up.** The amber pillar always points where to go next.
- **Buy something from Silas** at least once. Merchants keep records.
- **Not everything glints by daylight.**

*Rest well, keeper. Or don't rest at all.*
