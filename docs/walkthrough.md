# Last Rites: Walkthrough

> **Spoilers.** This page gives every answer in the game. Part A is spoiler-free; the stage parts solve the three cases. If you want to work out the mystery yourself, stop after Part A.

The game also guides you: the line at the top left is always your current objective, and a **pillar of amber light** with its name and distance stands over the place it means. The compass at the top shows the same marker. Directions below use the compass: **north** is towards Mausoleum Row, **south** is the way the hut door faces.

---

## Part A: How to play (no spoilers)

1. **Controls.** WASD to move, Shift to sprint, mouse to look. **E** interacts, **Left click** attacks, **Right click** blocks, **Q** kicks, **C** dodges, **V** heavy attack, **R** reloads. **1–4** pick a hotbar slot, **Tab** opens the satchel, **J** the journal, **M** the map, **N** toggles the minimap, **F** the lantern, **Esc** pauses. Every key can be changed in Settings.
2. **The loop.** Each of the 3 stages is one **day** and one **night**.
   - **By day** nothing can hurt you. Explore, gather materials, craft, and solve the stage's puzzles. A clock shows "Dusk in m:ss".
   - **At night** the curse holds back the sun. Zombies come in waves that **never stop** until you give the case's answer at the **chapel altar**.
3. **Your lantern is your light and your fuel gauge.** The flame on the HUD is your oil; as it burns down, your light shrinks. Pick up oil whenever you see it.
4. **Craft a weapon before your first dusk.** Go to the workbench in the hut (straight ahead when you wake). Recipes you can make **glow**. If you only have the shovel when dusk is 2 minutes away, the objective changes to "Craft a weapon at the workbench" to remind you.
5. **Clues and the case board.** Clues go into the journal (**J**, "Case" tab) as pinned cards. Join two cards that belong together with **red string**: drag from one to the other, or select one, select the other and press Enter. A right pair gets a thick solid string, a ✓ tag and a short deduction note, and lowers the **Curse meter**. A wrong pair costs nothing.
6. **Answering.** The final question sits at the bottom of the case board as a sentence with blanks. Fill it in any time, but you can only **give** it at the chapel altar (**E**, "Open") **at night**.
   - **Right:** the waves stop, the zombies fall to dust and the sun rises.
   - **Wrong:** a wave spawns at once and the altar is silent for 30 s. After 2 wrong answers you must link one more pair of clues before trying again.
7. **Hints.** Each case has 3 hints bought with Grave Coins (5 / 10 / 20): which cards matter, the rule, then the answer. In Story mode the third hint becomes **free** once the night has run 3 minutes, so you can never be stuck.
8. **Surviving the night.** Stay near the chapel. Board up its doors (front and vestry) so zombies can't pour in. Telegraphs warn you before every scare. If you die, **Retry Night** starts the same night again.
9. **Dawn.** After each night you pick an upgrade, and **Silas Vane**, the travelling merchant, sells supplies. **Buy something from him at least once**: his receipt is a clue later.
10. **Too scary?** Settings has scare intensity Full / Reduced / Off, Jumpscares, Flashing lights, Camera shake, Head bob and subtitles.

## Stage 1: "Saved by the Bell" (buried alive)

**Day 1**

1. Wake in the keeper's hut. **Read the page Elias left** on the table (journal page 1).
2. **Craft a weapon** at the workbench.
3. Take the **parish ledger page** from the hut shelf, beside the powder tin. This opens the **Ledger Cross-check**.
4. **Read the stones** north-west of the hut: **Marsh**, **Tobias Hale**, **John Pell**, **Martha Wren**, and to the north-east **Esther Ames** and **Samuel Dowd**. Each one you read adds its rubbing to the cross-check.
   - Journal page 2 lies near Hale's stone.
5. In the cross-check, draw a line from each stone to its ledger line and **flag the three mismatches**:
   1. **Pell: date.** The stone says 12 March 1862, the ledger 13 March.
   2. **Ames: certificate.** The ledger lists no death certificate.
   3. **Hale: plot.** The stone says west row 1, the ledger east row 4.
   - Marsh, Wren and Dowd match; flagging them says "These two agree".
   - This narrows the case to **3 graves**: Pell, Ames and Hale.
6. **Examine the three bells.** Each of those graves has a safety-bell post beside it. Turn the bell, inspect the rope and read the tag on each.
   - **Pell:** the rope is **cut clean by a blade**, and the tag says the certificate was signed **12 March 1862, the day before he died**.
   - **Ames:** the bell is **rusted solid**; it never rang.
   - **Hale:** everything is sound.
   - Name **Pell** as the cut bell.
7. **Ignore the red herrings:** fresh flowers on Hale's grave (a widow still visits) and Marsh's toppled stone (frost heave).
8. **Link the clues** on the case board (each right pair lowers the curse):
   1. No certificate + Wrong date
   2. The cut rope + The rusted bell
   3. The cut rope + The hasty tag
   4. No certificate + The rusted bell
   5. Wrong date + The hasty tag
9. **Fill in the final question:** "The one buried alive lies in **Pell's grave** because **the bell rope was cut and the certificate was signed before the death**."

**Night 1**

10. At dusk, go to the chapel (just east-south-east of the hut), board up the doors and fight.
11. At the **altar**, press **E** and give the answer. The waves stop and the sun rises.

**Dawn 1**

12. Pick an upgrade. **Buy something from Silas Vane** (keep his receipt).
13. **Dig Pell's grave:** it now holds the **Rusted Crypt Key**. That key opens the **Mausoleum Row gate** to the north-west.

## Stage 2: "The Resurrection Men" (body snatchers)

The suspects: the sexton, the doctor, the lodge keeper and the gravedigger.

**Day 2**

1. **Open the Mausoleum Row gate** with the Rusted Crypt Key.
2. **Read the six crypt name tablets** on the avenue railing:

   | Family | Crest | Laid down |
   |---|---|---|
   | Marlowe | Tower ♜ | 1752 |
   | Thorne | Leaf ❦ | 1758 |
   | Kestrel | Star ✦ | 1761 |
   | Ashby | Moon ☾ | 1767 |
   | Whitlock | Cross ✠ | 1803 |
   | Penrose | Swords ⚔ | 1811 |

3. **Open the sexton's crest lock** (a niche on the west side of the avenue). The note says "Turn them in the order the founders were laid down". Only the four founders' crests are on the dials, so set them left to right to **Tower, Leaf, Star, Moon**.
   - Inside: the **Sexton's Bone Whistle**, and the sexton's watch ledger is pinned as a clue.
4. **Put the watch log in order.** In the gatekeeper's lodge, just outside the Row gate, eight torn pages lie on the table. Order them from the night of 9 November 1849: **nine, ten, eleven, midnight, one, three, four, five**.
   - The log is empty **between one and three**, on the **lodge keeper's** watch, the night the Ames grave was robbed.
   - Also on the lodge table: the **surgeon's receipt** for "subjects", in the lodge keeper's hand.
   - The lodge holds a **shotgun**: take it.
5. **Compare the boot prints** at the Ames grave (north-east of the hut). Of the three plaster casts, the smudged heel is too worn and the narrow print is from town. Match the **full print** (cross-cut tread, size 11, a ring of nine hobnails with one missing at the heel) to the **lodge keeper's boots**.
   - The gravedigger's boots look the same but have a full ring of ten hobnails.
6. **Ignore the red herrings:** the spade with fresh clay just south of the hut (the gravedigger dug a lawful grave) and the doctor's anatomy-school letter in the avenue (he bought legally, with a licence).
7. **Link the clues:**
   1. The two-hour gap + Sexton's watch ledger
   2. Matching boots + The two-hour gap
   3. Surgeon's receipt + Matching boots
8. **Fill in the final question:** "The bodies were sold by **the lodge keeper**. Motive: **the surgeon's receipt in his hand**. Means: **the matching boots**. Opportunity: **the gap on his watch**."

**Night 2**

9. Hold the chapel and give the answer at the altar.
   - **Name the Dead** (optional, Stages 2 and 3): during waves one grave glows with a name. If you know that name from your clues, hold **E** there for 3 s to seal it for the wave (+100 score).

**Dawn 2**

10. Upgrade, and visit Silas if you haven't bought from him yet.

## Stage 3: "The Sealed Catacombs" (Father Crane)

**Day 3**

1. **The hymn board** in the chapel reads **4 · 19 · 2 · 41 · 23**. The **psalter** on the vestry cupboard lists hymns 1 to 60 by title. Crane's confession says "What I hid, I hid beneath the Word."
2. Look up those five hymns: their first letters spell **BELOW**. Set the five brass letter tiles on the altar to **B, E, L, O, W**, then **blow the Bone Whistle** at the altar. The **hidden stair** in the chapel opens.
   - This works at night too.
3. **Reconstruct Elias's last night.** Find the six evidence cards:
   - his **dry boots** in the hut
   - his **unsent letter** to the Bishop, on the hut's west table
   - **hearse-cart ruts** just inside the Row gate
   - **spilled oil** on the Crane crypt step, at the end of Mausoleum Row
   - the **iron pin in the hasp** of the Crane crypt door
   - **scratches** low on the same door
4. At the **slate** beside the Crane crypt, put the cards in order from midnight to dawn:
   1. dry boots
   2. unsent letter
   3. cart ruts
   4. spilled oil
   5. hearse-pin
   6. nail marks
   - The pin went in **before** the scratching: Elias was **sealed in alive**.
5. Pull the pin. The crypt opens: Elias's body and the **Iron Ossuary Key**.
6. **Gather the five relics of the rite.** The objective pillar points to the nearest one you've seen.
   - **Crane's Rosary:** inside the Crane crypt, Mausoleum Row.
   - **Sexton's Bell Clapper:** in the far north-east corner of the grounds.
   - **Child's Locket:** far west, on the smallest grave. It **only appears at night**.
   - **Burial Shroud:** in the ossuary, down the chapel stair.
   - **Reliquary Heart:** in the deepest catacomb chamber, behind the iron door. The Ossuary Key opens it.
   - Journal page 4 (beside the shroud) and page 5 (on the chapel lectern) give the rite order.
7. **Clues for the optional accusation.** Pick these up too:
   - the **Josiah Vane flyleaf** on the catacomb floor, past the scratch marks
   - the **Vane & Sons receipt** from your first purchase from Silas
   - The ledger, the flyleaf and the receipt are all in **the same careful hand**: father and son.
   - **Ignore the red herrings:** oil at a different crypt, and the old bishop's seal in the vestry.
8. **Link the clues:**
   1. Flyleaf name + Vane & Sons receipt
   2. Elias's letter + Flyleaf name
   3. Cart ruts + The hearse-pin
   4. Hymn letters + Elias's letter

**Night 3: the rite**

9. Go to the altar. **Father Crane rises** when the rite begins. Keep him and the waves off you.
10. **Place the relics in order** (the altar shows a line for each step):
    1. **Crane's Rosary:** "First the prayer"
    2. **Sexton's Bell Clapper:** "Then the bell"
    3. **Child's Locket:** "Then the smallest of them"
    4. **Burial Shroud:** "Then the cloth laid over the lost"
    5. **Reliquary Heart:** "Last the heart"
    - The right order **lifts the curse and wins the game**.
11. **The accusation (optional; it doesn't affect the win).** "Elias was sealed in by **Silas Vane**. Motive: **the handwriting**. Means: **the hearse-pin**. Opportunity: **the cart ruts**."
    - A right answer adds the epilogue and the achievement **Justice for Elias**.
    - A wrong one has no penalty; Silas's cart just rolls away at dawn.
12. The ending plays by itself: the keeper walks out of the south gate into the sunrise. The end.
