# Last Rites: Walkthrough

> **Spoilers.** This page gives every answer in the game. Part A is spoiler-free; the stage parts solve the three cases. If you want to work out the mystery yourself, stop after Part A.

The game also guides you: the line at the top left is always your current objective, and a **pillar of amber light** with its name and distance stands over the place it means. The italic line under the objective says why the step matters. The compass at the top shows the same marker. Directions below use the compass: **north** is towards Mausoleum Row, **south** is the way the hut door faces.

**Short on time?** Pick **Developer mode** on the New Game screen. It is the same game, but **]** skips the current step and **`** opens a panel where you can jump straight to stage 2 or 3.

---

## Part A: How to play (no spoilers)

1. **Controls.** WASD to move, Shift to sprint, mouse to look. **E** interacts, **Left click** attacks, **Right click** blocks, **Q** kicks, **C** dodges, **V** heavy attack, **R** reloads. **1–4** pick a hotbar slot, **Tab** opens the satchel, **J** the journal, **M** the map, **N** toggles the minimap, **F** the lantern, **Space** skips a line of the keeper's narration, **Esc** pauses. Every key can be changed in Settings.
2. **The loop.** Each of the 3 stages is one **day** and one **night**.
   - **By day** nothing can hurt you. Explore, gather materials, craft, and follow the stage's steps. A clock shows "Dusk in m:ss".
   - **At night** the curse holds back the sun. The dead come in waves that **never stop** until you give the case's answer at the **chapel altar**.
3. **Your lantern is your light and your fuel gauge.** The flame on the HUD is your oil; as it burns down, your light shrinks. Pick up oil whenever you see it.
4. **Craft a weapon before your first dusk.** Go to the workbench in the hut. Recipes you can make **glow**. If you only have the shovel when dusk is 2 minutes away, the objective changes to "Craft a weapon at the workbench" to remind you.
5. **The journal.** Everything you learn is written up on the journal's **Case** tab (**J**). When a stage's steps are done, the Case tab asks the case's question, and you type a name.
6. **Answering.** You can only **give** the answer at the chapel altar (**E**) **at night**.
   - **Right:** the waves stop, the dead fall to dust and the sun rises.
   - **Wrong:** a wave arrives at once and the altar is silent for 30 seconds. Then you can try again.
   - Done early? The altar's bell calls the dusk at once.
7. **Hints.** Each case has 3 hints bought with Grave Coins (5 / 10 / 20): where to look, what to check, then the answer. The third hint becomes **free** once the night has run 3 minutes, so you can never be stuck for good.
8. **Surviving the night.** Stay near the chapel. Board up its doors (front and vestry) so the dead can't pour in. Every scare is warned of first. If you die, **Retry Night** starts the same night again.
9. **Dawn.** After each night you pick an upgrade, and **Silas Vane**, the travelling undertaker, sells supplies by the south gate. **Buy something from him at least once**: his receipt is a clue later.
10. **Too scary?** Settings has scare intensity Full / Reduced / Off, Jumpscares, Flashing lights, Camera shake, Head bob and subtitles.

## Stage 1: "Saved by the Bell" (buried alive)

**Day 1**

1. Wake in the keeper's hut. **Read the page Elias left** on the table (journal page 1).
2. **Craft a weapon** at the workbench.
3. **Read the stones** of **John Pell**, **Esther Ames** and **Tobias Hale**. Pell and Hale are north-west of the hut, Ames to the north-east. Journal page 2 lies near Hale's stone.
4. **Check the ledger against the stones.** Take the parish ledger from the hut shelf. For each grave, mark whether the ledger agrees with its stone, and if not, what differs:
   - **Pell: the date.** The stone says 12 March 1862; the ledger says the 11th.
   - **Ames: the cause.** The stone says fever; the ledger says a fall.
   - **Hale: the plot.** The stone says west row 1; the ledger says east row 4.
   - Each changed entry is initialled **J.V.**
5. **Pull the bell cords** at the three graves. Each grave has a safety bell, so anyone buried alive could ring for help.
   - **Ames** and **Hale**: the bell rings.
   - **Pell**: the cord is **cut clean by a blade**, and the tag says the certificate was signed on **11 March 1862 by J. Vane, Undertaker**, a day before Pell died.
   - Find Pell's cut cord and the journal asks "Which bell was cut?" at once; you don't need the other two first. Answer **Pell**.
6. **Ignore the red herrings:** fresh flowers on Hale's grave (a widow still visits) and Marsh's toppled stone (frost heave).
7. **The question:** "Who silenced John Pell?" Answer **Josiah Vane** (or "J. Vane", or "the undertaker").

**Night 1**

8. At dusk, go to the chapel (just east-south-east of the hut), board up the doors and fight.
9. At the **altar**, press **E** and give the answer. The waves stop and the sun rises, and the altar gives up the **Rusted Crypt Key**.

**Dawn 1**

10. Pick an upgrade. **Buy something from Silas Vane** and keep his receipt.

## Stage 2: "The Resurrection Men" (body snatchers)

**Day 2**

1. **Open the Mausoleum Row gate** on the north fence, by the lodge, with the Rusted Crypt Key.
2. **Look at the three fresh graves** by the lodge: Ellen Cobb, Thomas Reade and Arthur Finch.
3. **Check them against the burial register** on the lodge table. **Arthur Finch's** grave, buried on the 14th, has loose soil and yellow clay on the grass, and his coffin is **empty**.
4. **Read the watch log** for the night of the 14th. Kerr, the lodge keeper, walks every round and Dale, the night porter, countersigns it. At **midnight and 2 am**, Kerr walked alone: **Dale never signed**.
5. **Take the boot print** from the mud at the foot of Finch's grave.
6. **Compare the boots** by the lodge table with the print. The visitor's have no nails and Dale's are worn flat. **Kerr's** have a full ring of hobnails and a sole cracked straight across: they match. A locker key falls out of one.
7. **Open Kerr's locker** at the west end of the lodge table. It has a Vane & Sons crest lock: turn it to the **raven on a coffin**, the same crest as the wax seal on John Pell's tag. Inside are a receipt (*Vane & Sons paid Kerr £12 for three "subjects", delivered to a surgeon*) and the **Sexton's Bone Whistle**.
8. **Ignore the red herrings:** the spade with fresh clay just south of the hut (the gravedigger dug a lawful grave) and the doctor's letter in the avenue (he bought legally, with a licence).
9. **The question:** "Who paid for the stolen bodies?" Answer **Vane & Sons** (or "the Vanes").

**Night 2**

10. Hold the chapel and give the answer at the altar.
    - **Name the Dead** (optional, Stages 2 and 3): during waves one grave glows with a name. If you know that name from your clues, hold **E** there for 3 s to seal it for the wave (+100 score).

**Dawn 2**

11. Upgrade, and visit Silas if you haven't bought from him yet.

## Stage 3: "The Sealed Catacombs" (Elias's last night)

**Day 3**

1. **Read the hymn board** in the chapel: **4 · 19 · 2 · 41 · 23**. The **psalter** on the vestry cupboard lists hymns 1 to 60 by title. The first letters of those five hymns spell **BELOW**.
2. Set the five brass letter tiles to **B, E, L, O, W**, then **blow the Bone Whistle**. The **hidden stair** behind the altar opens. (The whistle does nothing at the altar before stage 3.)
3. **Follow the cart ruts** from just inside the Row gate. A hearse cart came down the Row, and its ruts run to the **Crane crypt** at the far end.
4. **Open the Crane crypt door.** An iron **hearse pin stamped with the Vane raven** holds it shut from outside. Pull it. Elias is inside, and so is the **Iron Ossuary Key**.
5. **Search Elias.** He carried an **unsent letter** to the Bishop, and the **note** that called him to the crypt.
6. **Compare the handwriting** on the journal's Case tab. Lay four hands side by side: the parish ledger, the Vane & Sons receipt (from your purchase from Silas), the note, and Josiah Vane's signature on Pell's tag.
   - The ledger, the receipt and the note share **one careful hand** with a looped V. Josiah's is plain and shaky.
   - So the careful hand is not Josiah's. It is his son's: **Silas Vane**.
7. **Gather the five relics of the rite.** The objective's marker points to the nearest one.
   - **Crane's Rosary:** inside the Crane crypt, Mausoleum Row.
   - **Sexton's Bell Clapper:** in the far north-east corner of the grounds, under the fallen bell tower.
   - **Child's Locket:** far west, on the smallest grave. It **only appears at night**. If it is the last relic you need by day, the marker points at its grave and says to come back after dusk.
   - **Burial Shroud:** in the ossuary, down the chapel stair. Journal page 4 lies beside it.
   - **Reliquary Heart:** in the deepest catacomb chamber, behind the iron door. The Ossuary Key opens it.
   - Journal page 4 and page 5 (on the chapel lectern) give the rite's order.
8. **Ignore the red herrings:** oil at the Lowe crypt, and the old bishop's seal in the vestry.

**Night 3: the rite**

9. **The accusation (optional).** At the altar: "Who sealed Elias Thorne in the Crane crypt?" Answer **Silas Vane**. Just "Vane" gets the reply "Which Vane?". A wrong name has no penalty.
10. **Lay the relics in order** (the altar shows a line for each):
    1. **Crane's Rosary:** "First the prayer"
    2. **Sexton's Bell Clapper:** "Then the bell"
    3. **Child's Locket:** "Then the smallest of them"
    4. **Burial Shroud:** "Then the cloth laid over the lost"
    5. **Reliquary Heart:** "Last the heart"
11. **Father Crane rises** when the rite begins. Keep him and the waves off you. The right order **lifts the curse and wins the game**.
12. The ending plays by itself: the keeper walks out of the south gate into the sunrise.
